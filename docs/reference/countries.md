# Countries and Spain

The countries of ISO 3166-1 and a field for their codes; Spain's provinces, autonomous
communities, postal codes and tax IDs.
In `fastapi_alcazaba.countries` and `fastapi_alcazaba.countries.spain`.

In the guide: [Countries and Spain](../guide/countries.md). In an app: [the example](../examples/countries.md).
