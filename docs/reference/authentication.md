# Authentication strategies

`FastAPIAuth` and its strategies. In `fastapi_alcazaba.auth`; `JWTStrategy`, `OAuth2Strategy`,
`OIDCStrategy`, `SAMLStrategy` and `UnkeyStrategy` in `fastapi_alcazaba.auth.strategies.jwt`,
`.oauth2`, `.oidc`, `.saml` and `.unkey`, with their extras.

In the guide: [Strategies](../guide/authentication/index.md#strategies). In an app: [the example](../examples/auth_strategies/index.md).
